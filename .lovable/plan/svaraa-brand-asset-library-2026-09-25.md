# svaraa brand asset library

## What I’ll build
- Add a private-feeling but publicly accessible `/assets` page, linked nowhere from the main site.
- Present the library in clear groups: complete logo, emblem, wordmark, and presentation slides.
- Include transparent light and dark versions of the complete logo and emblem, plus transparent wordmark files suited to light and dark backgrounds.
- Create three polished 16:9 slides at 1920 × 1080: product introduction, features, and the seven svaraas.
- Add preview images and direct download actions for every file.

## Visual direction
- Preserve svaraa’s ivory, aubergine, muted-gold, serif-led identity.
- Use current product photography as the visual anchor and feature photography as supporting material.
- Keep slide wording restrained: one strong thought, minimal supporting labels, generous space, and no unsupported claims.
- Make each slide complete enough for a deck and composed cleanly enough to crop or animate later.

## Slide content
1. **Introduction**: “Wear the stillness you seek” with the pendant and wristband as the dominant product signal.
2. **Features**: “Made to belong, not to be learned” with a compact overview of prayer, reminders, familiar voice, memory, knowledge, protection, and play.
3. **Seven svaraas**: all seven names presented as one coherent daily practice, supported by the existing feature imagery.

## Technical details
- Produce downloadable PNG assets at their intended sizes, including 1920 × 1080 slide exports.
- Store page media through the project’s asset delivery system and keep downloadable master copies available in Files.
- Add route-specific title, description, social metadata, and prevent search indexing for `/assets`.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, all download links, transparency, image dimensions, and visual quality.
